# Sharpen

Before you start, answer three questions in a few lines each.

1. How would a top expert in this area do it? Name their sources, their methods, and how they would know the result is right.
2. What is the user really after? Look past the literal request to the result they need.
3. Is the task over-specified or under-specified? Drop constraints that do not serve the goal and add only the ones that do. Leave the user's choices open.

Fold in what makes the work better, not bigger. Always keep one outside check that the result is right, such as a test, a run, or a source. Do not narrate this framing. Then do the work.
