### Investigate

Read-only. The deliverable is a cited explanation or a recommendation, not a change.

1. GROUND the subsystem in question.
2. Produce the explanation in ground.md's shape: overview, key concepts, how it works, where things live, gotchas. For a decision between alternatives, add a tradeoffs table and a recommendation with your real judgment.
3. REPORT. `micro/unslop` on the prose.

No BUILD. If the investigation turns into a change, hand back and re-route to bug or feature.

**Reply:** the explanation or the recommendation. Push back when the premise is wrong.
