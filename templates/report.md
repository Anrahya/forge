## For the consumer

What changes for the end user or the caller, before any implementation detail.

## For the maintainer

What the next engineer inherits. New structure, new invariants, new commands.

## What changed

Short list with evidence pointers. Every claim ends with `evidence: <path>`.

## Verification

Commands run and their outcome, with paths to red.log, green.log, verify.log, and any runtime evidence.

## Not done

What was left out or could not be verified, and why.

## Risks

What could go wrong after merge and how it would be noticed.

## Principles applied

Each principle name and the decision it changed. Skip any that changed nothing.
